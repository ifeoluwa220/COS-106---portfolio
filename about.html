🔗 From Quantity Surveying to Cybersecurity

How My Background Powers My Cybersecurity Career Goal

At first glance, Quantity Surveying and Cybersecurity seem completely different, but they share a core DNA: Risk Management, Precision, and Asset Protection.

  • Risk & Cost Assessment: In Quantity Surveying, I learned how to evaluate financial risk, audit resources, and prevent budget overruns. In cybersecurity, this translates seamlessly into vulnerability assessment and risk analysis—identifying where a digital system is exposed and estimating the impact of a breach.
  • Detailed Structural Integrity: A quantity surveyor carefully inspects blueprints to ensure a building has no structural weaknesses. As an aspiring Cybersecurity Analyst, I apply that exact same meticulous mindset to source code, network architectures, and web technologies (like COS 106) to catch flaws before malicious actors do.
  • Project & Resource Governance: Managing projects in the construction and physical asset sector taught me compliance, regulatory frameworks, and rigorous documentation—skills that are critical when implementing cybersecurity policies (like ISO 27001 or GDPR) for organizations.

🚀 Career Aspirations

My goal is to become a certified Cybersecurity Analyst and Risk Assessor. By combining my background in structural evaluation and risk management with modern web and network defense skills from MIVA, I aim to help enterprises secure their digital assets with the same precision applied to physical engineering projects.
